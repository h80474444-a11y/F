Dr. Tariq Aziz

Associate Professor (Soil Science)
University of Agriculture, Faisalabad (UAF)

Dr. Aziz serves as the Regional Director for South Asia at the International Nitrogen Initiative (INI) and is the lead partner from Pakistan in the global "South Asia Nitrogen Hub". With over 120 research articles, his work focuses on improving fertilizer use efficiency, nutrient recycling, and pollution mitigation.
